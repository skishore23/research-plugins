# Comfy Story Planner privacy policy

Publisher: Kishore Shimikeri. Effective date: October 1, 2026.

This policy covers the Comfy Story Planner skills package and its supported script entry point, not the separate upstream application.

## Processing and storage

The supported commands process user-selected inputs in the environment supplied by the agent host. They do not send inputs or outputs to the publisher, collect credentials, register accounts, or include publisher analytics or telemetry. Results are printed to the host; WorldZero may save traces to a user-selected new directory. There is no publisher-operated input or results database for these plugins.

ChatGPT, Codex or another host may process conversations, files and command outputs on its own services under its settings and privacy terms. Offline script execution does not mean that a conversation stays on your device. Users manage inputs, generated files and conversations through their filesystem and host controls. Installing dependencies may contact the configured package index. Broader upstream capabilities included in source are outside the supported script workflow.

## Website and support

This website uses GitHub Pages and no publisher analytics, cookies, external fonts or form collection. GitHub may process access and account information under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Support uses public GitHub Issues at https://github.com/skishore23/research-plugins/issues. If you post, your GitHub identity and submitted content can be visible to the publisher and the public. Support content is used to understand and respond to reported issues. Use synthetic examples and never post credentials, private media, personal data or confidential policies.

GitHub stores support posts under its controls; these plugins impose no separate automatic deletion schedule or support upload service. Use GitHub's controls to manage your posts or reference an existing issue number when requesting help. Questions about this policy can be raised through the support page without including sensitive data.
